# Guangzhou City Guide Character Manifest

Status: POSE LOCKED BEFORE GENERATION

## City research and identity

- Contemporary Guangzhou is a warm, humid, fast-moving southern metropolis shaped by Lingnan arcades, riverside public space, old Xiguan neighborhoods, trade, design and everyday street life.
- Character identity: a confident young local cultural-route editor who moves comfortably between historic neighborhoods and the modern Pearl River axis.
- Avoid costume shorthand: no invented ethnic dress, opera costume, lion-dance costume, or overloaded food props.
- Core design: short softly waved dark hair; light sage short-sleeve overshirt over a warm cream top; loose dark-teal ankle trousers; compact rust-red crossbody bag; folded rain umbrella as a practical subtropical-city prop.
- Palette: sage, cream, dark teal and restrained lychee red.

## Silhouette lock

- Narrow, composed stance rather than another wide or one-foot-forward stance.
- Folded umbrella creates one long low diagonal on the right side; the left elbow remains compact at the bag strap.
- Face and torso both orient left, unlike the existing counter-twist characters.
- Low-profile loafers must read differently from sneakers, flats, walking shoes and ankle boots already in the batch.

## System constraints

- Preserve the locked 4–4.5-head hand-drawn pen character system, simplified facial language, line weight and color behavior.
- Full-body transparent PNG with no ground plane, no lettering and no background block.
- Do not move both hands to the chest center.
- Default map-scale preview: approximately 14% of the map canvas height.

## Cross-city intent

This signature adds a centered narrow stance, same-direction head/torso orientation, asymmetric umbrella silhouette and loafers. It must not read as the Beijing pocket pose, Shanghai camera pose, Suzhou strap-only pose, Chengdu pointing pose, Chongqing transit-card pose or Guilin walking pose.

## City research and identity

- Shenzhen combines a young design/technology metropolis, a long subtropical coast, migrant everyday life, civic parks, creative-industry districts and much older settlements such as Nantou and Dapeng.
- Identity: a young public-space designer doing a walking audit between civic architecture, creative parks and the waterfront.
- Core design: straight chin-length dark bob tucked behind one ear; pale sky-blue sleeveless utility vest over a white short-sleeve tee; charcoal knee-length culottes; small cobalt waist pouch; slim rolled site plan as the only prop.
- Palette: sky blue, white, charcoal and one cobalt accent. No generic neon-tech costume and no invented regional dress.
